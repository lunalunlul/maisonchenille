The house

Born on a kitchen table

Maison Chenille was born in Paris, from a simple wish: to give flowers that stay. Chenille wire, soft and a little retro, lends itself beautifully.

What we do

Chenille-wire bouquets, shaped by hand by artisans who practise the gestures of ronghua — press, trim, assemble — then composed here in Paris, in our colours. Each bouquet is thought of as a small object: shades you won't find everywhere, a box you keep.

Kits to make them yourself will follow: the wire, the pattern and the gestures.

Where we come from

Chenille wire descends from a very old gesture: the silk flowers of the Chinese imperial courts, worn in the hair on the days that matter. We keep its intention — a flower you don't throw away — and a word, chenille, that made the same journey we did. Read the story →

The first bouquets

They were composed on a kitchen table, one autumn evening in 2026. The first ones live on the tables of a few friends' restaurants in Paris. If you come across one, it's ours.
