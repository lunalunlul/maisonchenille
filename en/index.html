The house

Born on a kitchen table

Maison Chenille was born in Paris from a simple wish: to make flowers with your hands, and keep them. Pipe cleaner, soft and a little retro, is perfect for it.

Each kit is designed as a small object: colours you won't find everywhere, a box you keep, a tutorial you follow without stress.

The first bouquets were made on a kitchen table, one autumn evening in 2026.
