Our story

A thousand years before chenille wire

Chenille wire descends from ronghua, the silk flowers of the Chinese imperial courts: silk clamped between two copper wires, pressed, trimmed, assembled with tweezers, worn in the hair on the days that matter. We give it back its gestures, in today's colours.
