The house

Born on a kitchen table

Maison Chenille was born in Paris from a simple wish: to give flowers that stay. Chenille wire, soft and a little retro, is perfect for it.

It descends from a very old gesture: ronghua, the silk flowers of the Chinese imperial courts. Silk threads twisted between two copper wires, pressed, trimmed, assembled with tweezers, then worn in the hair on the days that matter. We give it back its gestures, with today's colours.

Each bouquet is designed as a small object: colours you won't find everywhere, a box you keep. Kits to make them yourself will follow.

The first bouquets were composed on a kitchen table, one autumn evening in 2026.
